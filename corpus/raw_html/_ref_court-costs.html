Court Costs

In-Person Payments

In-Person payments may be made for limited transactions including marriage license, placing a will on deposit, or copies. Plan to arrive no later than 4:00 p.m. if your business requires payment.

Payment can be made by cash or any of the following payment methods.

All credit/debit transactions are subject to a 2.5% transaction fee with a minimum fee of $2.00.
All transaction fees are Non-Refundable.

E-Filing Payments

All payments for E-Filing must be made with a credit or debit card and are subject to a 3% transaction fee.
All transaction fees are Non-Refundable.
